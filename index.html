Rolling: Video Teleprompter

Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Rolling: Video Teleprompter ("Rolling", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Android app.

1. LOCAL CONTENT

Rolling is designed to keep your creative content on your device. Scripts, original recordings, edited recordings, thumbnails, and app preferences are stored in the app's private storage. We do not upload your scripts, video, or audio to our servers.

When you choose Export or Save to device, Rolling creates a copy in your device's media library. That exported copy is then managed by your device and any backup or synchronization service you have enabled.

Android backup is disabled for Rolling. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data can permanently delete content that you have not exported.

2. CAMERA, MICROPHONE, AND MEDIA ACCESS

Rolling requests camera and microphone access so you can preview the camera and record video with sound. These permissions are used only while you use recording features. Rolling cannot remotely activate your camera or microphone.

Rolling uses Android's media APIs to save recordings that you explicitly export. It does not scan or import your photo or video library.

3. SUBSCRIPTIONS AND PAYMENTS

Premium subscriptions are sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes your payment and manages your payment method. Rolling does not receive or store your full card number, bank details, or payment security code.

We use RevenueCat to provide subscription status and restore purchases. RevenueCat may process purchase history, subscription status, an anonymous app user identifier, transaction information, device and operating-system information, locale, currency, and related timestamps. RevenueCat does not receive your scripts, recordings, video, or audio from Rolling.

Learn about RevenueCat's data practices at RevenueCat Privacy Policy and Google's data practices at Google Privacy Policy.

If you choose Review access in Settings, Rolling sends the review access code and your anonymous RevenueCat app user identifier over HTTPS to our Cloudflare-hosted service to validate access and grant a complimentary Premium entitlement. Cloudflare processes connection information, including your IP address, to deliver and protect this service. We do not intentionally log reviewer codes or identifiers, and Rolling does not save the code on your device. No scripts, recordings, video, or audio are sent through this flow. Learn about Cloudflare's practices at https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/.

4. SUPPORT COMMUNICATIONS

If you contact us, we receive the information you voluntarily include in your message, such as your email address, device details, and description of the issue. We use it only to respond, provide support, and protect the app from misuse.

5. ANALYTICS AND ADVERTISING

Rolling does not include third-party advertising or behavioral advertising SDKs. The current app does not use a third-party analytics SDK. Google Play and RevenueCat may provide aggregated operational or purchase information under their own policies.

6. SHARING

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only as needed with:

  • Google, to process purchases and distribute the app;
  • RevenueCat, to manage subscription entitlements;
  • Cloudflare, to host and protect optional reviewer access;
  • our email provider, when you contact support; or
  • authorities or other parties when required by law or necessary to protect rights and safety.

7. RETENTION AND DELETION

Local app content remains on your device until you delete it, clear app data, or uninstall Rolling. Exported copies remain in your media library until you delete them there.

Google and RevenueCat retain transaction and subscription records according to their legal obligations and privacy policies. Support emails are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the request, maintain records, or comply with law. You may request deletion of support information by contacting us.

8. SECURITY

Subscription information is transmitted using encrypted network connections. No storage or transmission method is completely secure, and you are responsible for protecting access to your device and exported recordings.

9. CHILDREN

Rolling is a general-audience productivity app and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

10. YOUR CHOICES

You can deny or revoke camera and microphone permissions in Android settings, although recording will not work without them. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play. You can delete local content inside the app or by clearing app data.

11. CHANGES

We may update this policy when the app or applicable requirements change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.

12. CONTACT

For privacy questions or requests, contact rolling.official.app@gmail.com.
